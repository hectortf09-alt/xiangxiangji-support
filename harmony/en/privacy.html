1. Scope and contact

Boxmory is provided by individual developer Tengfei He. This policy describes information processing in the offline HarmonyOS app.

Contact: tengfei.he@aliyun.com.

2. Information processed on your device

The app stores content you enter or select: projects, boxes, items, names, quantities, tags, ordering, manually entered locations, photos and thumbnails, unassigned drafts, recognition suggestions and operation receipts. Recent searches and entry suggestions are stored locally.

Photo assistance uses a model bundled with the app. Image analysis and cropping take place on the device without cloud recognition or image uploads. Suggestions are editable and are not saved as boxed items until you confirm. No GPS location is collected.

This content is used for recording, searching, organizing, exports and backups. It is not automatically sent to us or used for advertising, sale, profiling or tracking. Interface translations are bundled and work offline.

3. Permissions and files

  • Camera: the system camera opens only when you choose to take a photo. If it is unavailable, you can enter text or select a photo.
  • Photos: the system picker supplies only images you select. The app does not scan your library or change originals. Cloud-only photos may be downloaded by the selected photo provider according to your settings.
  • Clipboard: when you tap the system Paste button in the intake panel, clipboard text is used to create candidate drafts on your device.
  • Files: you choose files and destinations in the system picker or share sheet for imports and exports.
  • Available storage: the app may check local free space before file operations. This information is not sent to us.

You can cancel a picker at any time. The app does not request contacts, microphone or device location access for its current features.

4. Exports, backups and sharing

You initiate all exports and sharing and choose recipients and destinations. PDF and CSV lists contain the selected scope of saved records; full backups include records, photos and unassigned drafts. Executable undo state is not transferred through a backup.

Cloud drives, email, messaging apps or other recipients process the files you send under their own policies. This is not an upload to the developer. You can transfer records between devices with a manual backup.

Keep a current full backup before importing, replacing or restoring records. Replacement can overwrite the selected local data. PDF and CSV lists cannot restore app data.

5. Retention, deletion and choices

Records and photos are kept in the app sandbox. You can view, edit or delete records, remove photos, discard drafts, clear recent searches, delete projects, or export a backup. Recent entry suggestions are limited to each project and are removed when that project is deleted.

Deleting the app and its data removes local app data. We have no server copy and cannot recover lost local records.

Deleting app data does not delete original gallery photos, files already exported elsewhere or system backups. Manage those copies in their respective locations. The HarmonyOS app provides manual full backups in Settings.

No account deletion step is needed because there are no accounts. Remove local records or stop using the app at any time. Contact us about support emails as described below.

6. Components and system services

minizip-ng handles archives locally. MindSpore Lite runs the bundled YOLOv3-Tiny model on your device. License information is available in the app. There are no third-party ads, analytics, social sign-in or tracking SDKs, and no developer service collecting identifiers, usage events or diagnostic logs.

Distribution, system photo selection, sharing and diagnostics are governed by the relevant platform and device settings. File recipients have their own policies.

7. When you contact support

If you email tengfei.he@aliyun.com, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments or device/version details you choose to include. We use them to answer questions, investigate issues and handle your request, not for advertising or sale.

Send only what is necessary. Redact private content in screenshots; do not send full backups, identity documents or private photos. Communications are retained only as needed to handle support and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion by email; requests will be verified and handled as applicable.

Your email provider and our mailbox provider also process email under their own rules. The app does not read your mailbox or automatically send email or attachments.

8. Public web pages

Public support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or submission forms. Local bundled copies can be read without visiting the hosted page.

When visiting hosted pages, GitHub logs IP addresses for security. This comes from a web visit, not uploading storage records or photos. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

9. Data security

The app uses the system sandbox. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep full backups in a trusted location and choose file recipients carefully. Exports can contain private photos and location descriptions.

Device loss, system failures, damaged storage and accidental deletion may cause data loss. We cannot guarantee absolute security or remotely access or delete your local copies.

10. Children

The app does not seek children's personal information or require age or identity details. Guardians should help minors manage photo permissions, exports and support communications. Contact the email above with questions about information voluntarily provided to us.

11. Policy updates

If data processing changes, this policy and its date will be updated, with notices in the app or store as applicable. Any future online, account or sync feature will have appropriate information and any legally required consent before processing begins. Features not provided are not represented as active processing.
